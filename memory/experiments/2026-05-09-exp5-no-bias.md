# Exp-5: No-bias transformer blocks

**Date:** 2026-05-09
**Phase:** 2
**Branch / commit:** `feature/sota-upgrade-roadmap` @ `92fd52b` (post-Exp-4 defaults plus mid-phase progress note); runs produced at this SHA.

## Hypothesis

Removing biases should be close to neutral under the current Phase 2 defaults because GPT-2 initialization, residual paths, and LayerNorm already carry most affine calibration. The removed bias tensors are tiny relative to the matmul weights and should not materially affect throughput or VRAM. Prediction before running: marginal val_loss versus Exp-4 should land within about +/-1%, with a slight chance of a small regularization-like win on B1/B3 and no confident direction on B2.

## Setup

Single flag flip: `use_bias=false` (was `true`). The post-Exp-4 defaults stayed active: `attention_scale_mode="head"`, `ffn_activation="gelu"`, `tie_weights=false`, and `init_scheme="gpt2"`.

## Interpretation

The hypothesis was directionally too optimistic. The effect is small, but it is a consistent regression on all three benchmarks: +0.60% B1, +0.19% B2, and +0.28% B3 marginal val_loss versus Exp-4. That uniform direction matters more than the small absolute deltas because the flag has no compensating readability or speed advantage.

The likely mechanism is that the remaining biases still provide useful cheap affine correction even with GPT-2 initialization and LayerNorm in place. In this codebase `use_bias=false` removes bias from the MHA output projection, both FFN Linear layers, the two per-block LayerNorms, the final block norm, and the output head. The parameter count reduction is tiny, and the removed degrees of freedom are exactly the sort of low-cost shifts that can help each benchmark calibrate logits under a fixed 5000-step budget.

The result is still close to the "small/flat" prediction in magnitude. The miss is the sign: the regularization-like upside did not show up, and the affine-capacity cost was visible enough to be consistent across domains.

## Conclusion

Do **not** ship `use_bias=false` as the default. Keep `use_bias=true`; leave the flag available for future ablations or for users who want to match bias-free transformer variants. There is no config change from Exp-5.

## Open questions

- Would a longer budget erase the small regression? Possibly, but Phase 2 decisions are scoped to the standard 5000-step contract.
- Does no-bias interact differently with tied weights under GPT-2 init? Exp-6 includes `use_bias=false` as part of the full stack, so it will test the combined interaction, but not isolate the bias flag cleanly.
- Is the +0.19% to +0.60% regression above seed noise? Single-seed Phase 2 cannot answer that. The uniform direction across three benchmarks makes the default-flip rejection sufficient, but a multi-seed Phase 3 variance run would quantify it.
